# Changelog

All notable changes to NFL Blitz 2027. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-06

First public release (build 1006a). Everything below describes how NFL Blitz 2027 differs from the original **NFL Blitz 2000 Gold** arcade game (MAME blitz2k).

### Teams
- **32 franchises.** The original shipped with 31 teams and no Houston; the Houston Texans are a full team with their own roster, ratings, helmets, uniforms, end zone, VS-screen wordmark and city plate, team-select emblems, on-field name tags and announcer bank.
- **Alphabetical team select**, with Houston in place and the relocated teams listed under their current cities.
- **Relocations and rebrands** read as their modern selves everywhere (matchup screen, wordmarks, emblems, ratings, operator menu): Oakland Raiders → Las Vegas Raiders, San Diego Chargers → Los Angeles Chargers, St. Louis Rams → Los Angeles Rams, Washington Redskins → Washington Commanders.

### Rosters and ratings
- **Current players on every team.** The nine named starters per team (QB, RB, two receivers/TE, two defensive backs, linebacker, kicker) are current NFL players, from the official depth charts as of 2026-10-02.
- **Team strength re-rated for all 32 teams** across the five team-select categories (Passing, Rushing, Linemen, Defense, Special Teams), from completed 2025-season metrics plus 2026 offseason moves.
- **On-field name tags and team-select cameos** show the current players.

### Announcer
- **New announcer voice on every team-name callout** and on roughly 300 player-name callouts, so the announcer calls today's players. Houston has its own callouts.
- **Natural-speed delivery.** Every callout plays out in full; the matchup screen waits for the longer team names.
- **Correct pronunciations (hopefully)** for today's names, including suffixes (Jr., II, III).
- **Spoken year references** in the welcome and trivia intros updated.

### Artwork and branding
- **Team-select emblems and wordmarks** for all 32 teams in current team art.
- **Home and away uniforms** for every team.
- **VS-screen helmets** and **attract-mode champion helmets** for all 32 teams with current logos.
- **End-zone plaques** for all 32 teams.
- **Win banners** use each team's wordmark; dark wordmarks carry a white outline for contrast.
- **Title screen, attract-mode "NEW FEATURES" page, boot information page and version text** rebranded to BLITZ 2027 (in-game version string reads 2.0).

### Gameplay and operator menu
- **Grand Champion** award and record screens count all 32 teams.
- **Operator/service menu** is reachable; settings save and survive a restart. Free Play and Clock Speed take effect in-game. Difficulty and Violence save but, as in the original game's code, have no effect.

### Trivia
- **Blitz Trivia Challenge rewritten**: all 930 question slots carry new, fact-checked questions.

### Unchanged
- Gameplay, physics, plays and controls are the original's. The ROM set (blitz2k.zip) is untouched; only the disk image changes.
